About

Building resilience intelligence for the built environment

Dr. Milad Roohi is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Charles W. Durham School of Architectural Engineering and Construction at UNL and director of the SiRIUS Lab. Before UNL, he was a Senior Scientist at Aon Impact Forecasting and a postdoctoral fellow at the NIST Center for Risk-Based Community Resilience Planning. He earned his Ph.D. from the University of Vermont.

  • NIST Senior Research Associate (PREP) · Project IN-CORE Community Resilience Specialist
  • Adjunct Faculty, Utah State University · NSRI Academic Network Member
  • Prior: Aon Impact Forecasting · NIST CoE postdoc · Ph.D., University of Vermont
  • Lab site with people, funding, and testbeds: sirius.unl.edu
