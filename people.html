MR

Milad Roohi, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor, Durham School of Architectural Engineering and Construction · Director, SiRIUS Lab · College of Engineering Teaching Fellow

Dr. Milad Roohi is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Charles W. Durham School of Architectural Engineering and Construction at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln and director of the SiRIUS Lab. Before joining UNL, he worked as a Senior Scientist at Aon Impact Forecasting and completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the NIST Center for Risk-Based Community Resilience Planning. He earned his Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from the University of Vermont.

He specializes in structural engineering and mechanics; risk, reliability, and resilience engineering; and infrastructure sensing and monitoring. He chairs the ASCE EMI Objective Resilience Committee and is a member of the EERI Early Career Committee.
